09 Questions
Exact live FAQ copy
1. What is CHOICE iD?
   CHOICE iD puts everything you can do in one place: your CV, work accounts, diplomas, courses and certificates, each confirmed by the place that gave it to you. You find real jobs, get a CV made for each one, and share proof with a link you can switch off. It is built by CHOICE Foundation and free to start.
2. Where can I find jobs that really fit me?
   CHOICE iD shows one open list of remote and local jobs from EURES (the public job services of 31 European countries), from TED public tenders, and from company career pages on Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Personio, SmartRecruiters and Workable. Everyone sees the same list. We tell you in words, never with a number, why a job may fit you.
3. I don't know which job is right for me. Can you help?
   Yes. Taila asks you a few questions and helps you see what you are good at. Soon your Job Map will show the roles that fit your story, and a plan to get there.
4. How can an employer check my proof without my documents?
   You send a link made for that one employer. It opens only the items you chose. Each one says who confirmed it and when, and has a signature anyone can check. The employer keeps nothing, and you switch the link off when you want.
5. Can I prove a diploma or a certificate?
   Yes. The school or body that gave it confirms it, and it is matched to you, not to a scan. Certificates saved on a blockchain are matched to your digital wallet. A photo of a paper is never accepted, because nobody can check it.
6. Does CHOICE iD give people a score?
   No. Nobody gets a score, a rating or a rank. Employers cannot search or browse people, and there is no list of candidates. This is a rule we built in, not a setting.
7. Is my data safe?
   Your profile is private until you share it. Each link is for one person, and you can switch it off any time. You can delete everything in one step. We build to the strictest privacy rules in the world (Europe's GDPR), for people everywhere.
8. Does a machine decide anything about me?
   No. A writing helper puts your facts into a CV and a letter, and suggests skill words from what you said. It never scores or ranks you, and nothing is saved until you say yes.
9. Who is Taila?
   Taila is your guide inside CHOICE iD. Tap once and talk to her like a video call: she listens, answers in her own voice in 12 languages and helps with your next step. She tells you first that she is an AI and can make mistakes. She never scores you and never decides for you.
10. What is CHOICE, the coin?
    CHOICE is our community coin. You earn it by learning, playing and doing community tasks. It is a thank-you, never a rating: it is never shown to an employer as proof and never used to compare people.
11. Can a recruitment agency use CHOICE iD?
    Yes. Agencies get the same links as employers: confirmed items, each with who and when, and nothing to store. Coming soon: agencies and employers can post their own jobs to the same open list, clearly labelled.
