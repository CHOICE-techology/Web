# Brand confirmations + plan amendments (23 Nov 2026)

Source: CHOICE_iD_Brand_Guidelines_October_2026.pdf (authoritative). Plan approved by Alexander 23 Nov with amendments below.

## Brand book, confirmed
- Typography: Poppins (hero, headers, buttons, logo), Nunito Regular/Bold (lead and body per type hierarchy), Merriweather Sans secondary. NOTE: live site body is Public Sans, the book says Nunito. Needs Alexander's call before build.
- Colour roles: White = canvas/space/clarity; Black = message/structure/contrast; Grey #545454 = CHOICE/utility/context; Neon Cyan #34FAFB = CHOICE iD signal/relationship only, never wallpaper. Pink #FF008A = CHOICE DAO provenance only, never on the CHOICE iD site.
- UI: corners 8 px; spacing 8/16/24/40/64; hover brightens with cyan; keyboard focus 2 px black outline, never removed.
- Motion: TRACE, CONNECT, REVEAL, RELEASE. "If the animation can be removed without losing meaning, simplify it." Digital layers reveal a relationship, not an effect.
- Product surfaces: no decorative UI, no invented product states. Truth guardrails p42 apply to Scene 10 copy.
- Icons: Phosphor Icons (used for evidence tiles).
- Logo: no effects, rotation, recolour; once per surface.
