# Brand amendments, 26 Nov 2026 (DRAFT, from Alexander's words)

Status: recorded from Alexander's message of 26 Nov. Items marked OPEN need his confirmation. This file continues docs/BRAND-CONFIRMATIONS-AND-AMENDMENTS-23NOV.md and is meant to be merged into the brand book when approved.

## Working rule
The brand book is a living document until the platform is finished. Every new approved approach is recorded here first, so everything can later be transferred into the brand book. New ideas improve the chosen direction. They never replace it.

## Visual material direction (Alexander, 26 Nov)
- Clean, minimalist, cinematic, animated 3D website that gives visitors a wow effect.
- White, transparent volumes with a soft luminous quality, "like a neon balloon, but not much". The aim is volume and depth.
- Few colours. Interest comes from the objects and from materials that move, not from colour.
- Entertainment and clarity together: nothing makes the visitor work harder to understand CHOICE iD.

## OPEN, to confirm
1. "Neon balloon": read as a soft inner glow and a glossy, inflated feel in white. Does it allow any colour beyond the thin cyan #34FAFB signal? Current rule: cyan only as a thin signal, no blue, purple or pink.
2. Glow and glass must not return to heavy blur. The 23 Nov brief says no excess blur.
3. Which material change is allowed on the existing models (ribbon, monolith, tiles) without changing geometry.
4. Where references conflict with the rules (blue and purple glass, green check marks), only technique may be borrowed, never colour.
